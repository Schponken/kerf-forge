# E-check: check a design Kerf did not make

A shelf bracket modelled in another CAD tool (STEP). Kerf must check it without designing anything: import the model,
print checks in the declared orientation, mass, and FEA of a 3 kg shelf load at the arm tip with the plate held at its
two wall screws, judged against the layer-direction strength for that orientation (`kerf run <p> --mode check`).
