# Independent reviews, 2026-11-01

Two fresh reviewers (no context from the build sessions) on Spencer's request: "assume other people will use this; what
would they complain about; how could it be smarter" and "not just simple parts slapped together; every design choice
should have a purpose". Condensed; PLAN.md Phase 6 holds the work items.

## A. CAD depth and design intent

Analysis is deep (sourced load cases, e.g. robot_arm/project.yaml:177-315); geometry is shallow: Box/Cylinder unions,
constant sections, fillets added only where a check failed. FEA was used only as pass/fail.

Grades: robot_arm C, fpv_5in C+, pan_tilt B−, servo_link C, tvc_drone C−, bionic_finger B (mechanism) / C (form).

Telling examples:
- robot_arm forearm/upper_arm: identical 7×34 mm plates + two 4 mm webs (open fork, weak in torsion); FEA 1.9 of 24 MPa
  (12× margin), base 1.2 of 13, yoke_l 0.8 of 13; `yoke()` and `plate()` copy-pasted between files; finger is a flat
  6 mm slab with no grip surface; base has 77 sharp inside edges.
- fpv_5in: arm tapers in plan (good) but constant 11 mm depth; key ridges' roots unfilleted; tie slots at mid-depth
  (peak shear); body 206 sharp inside edges; arm at 95 % of its deflection limit (needs section/detail, not less material).
- pan_tilt: best finish (teardrop knuckles, gussets, reliefs, labels, knock cases at 1.3–1.5×), still slab-built.
- tvc_drone: ring_low ~12 Box unions, ribs bolted on after FEA failures; gimbal_outer flat ring + box pads.
- bionic_finger: strong joint/tendon intent; every segment a rectangle (no taper, no pad).

Systemic causes: FEA pass/fail only (no utilisation or field feedback); no section-shape helpers; detail pass is prose
with no gate and `safe_fillet` silently skips; no record of why a feature exists; box stubs become the parts; random
radii (0.3–8 mm), no design language.

Recommended engine changes (ranked): stress-field feedback (done: kerf/stressmap.py); sharp-corner check (done:
kerf/edges.py, warning); beam/fork-link section generators; feature-why ledger; detail-pass gate + project radius set;
per-part intent scores in review (done: cli.intent_scores); Load → section table before geometry (done: kerf skill);
idiom library. Showcase reworks: robot_arm links, gripper/finger, base; fpv arm.
