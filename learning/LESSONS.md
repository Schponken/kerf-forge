# Lessons learned (Learning Mode)

Rebuilt by `kerf lookback` from the diaries in learning/diary/. A proposed gate is a PROPOSAL for Spencer to approve; nothing here is enforced.
Projects read: bionic_finger, check_shelf_bracket, drone_foldable_wings, fpv_5in, fpv_wing, pan_tilt, robot_arm, robotic_gripper_claw, robotic_gripper_desk, servo_link, tvc_drone.

## Proposed gates (need Spencer's approval)

- [strength] Size load-carrying parts from their loads (section, material, safety factor) before detail drawing. (82x in 10 projects, e.g. bionic_finger, drone_foldable_wings, fpv_5in; catchable at rough). Would catch it: the *_fea / fea_static / cantilever_stress requirements run on the rough parts (stress checks are paused in this repo, see PAUSED.json: until resumed, a hand check of the section in DESIGN.md).
- [process] Update DESIGN.md, ASSEMBLY notes and the BOM in the same commit as the decision they record. (69x in 9 projects, e.g. bionic_finger, drone_foldable_wings, fpv_5in; catchable at detail). Would catch it: a planning step: DESIGN.md requirements table and part list re-read at each milestone.
- [print] Choose print orientation, supports and plate layout when each part is drawn, from its load direction and overhangs. (29x in 9 projects, e.g. drone_foldable_wings, fpv_5in, fpv_wing; catchable at detail). Would catch it: the print checks (overhang, bed fit, layer direction) in `kerf check`, read per part as it is drawn.
- [process] Review before the end: run the design reviewer (or at least its checks) on the contract and the rough parts, then again after detail, not only at the final review. (9x in 9 projects, e.g. bionic_finger, drone_foldable_wings, fpv_5in; catchable at rough). Would catch it: a planning step: a design-reviewer pass on the DESIGN.md contract + rough assembly, and `kerf check` at every part-group milestone.
- [access/fasteners] Check a key or screwdriver can reach every screw, and every insert and nut is placed, when the fastener is first drawn. (36x in 8 projects, e.g. bionic_finger, drone_foldable_wings, fpv_5in; catchable at detail). Would catch it: hex_key_reach, repair_access and connector_access run per part group; ball-end keys listed in ASSEMBLY.md.
- [fit/collision] Run the collision and clearance checks on the rough assembly and after every part group; fix the part that owns the overlap. (13x in 7 projects, e.g. bionic_finger, drone_foldable_wings, fpv_5in; catchable at rough). Would catch it: `kerf check` (interference, clearance_min, prop_clearance) after each part group instead of once at the end.
- [motion/assembly] Prove each moving part's full travel, its stop and its over-travel on the rough assembly (servo throw, horn, linkage), not after detail. (26x in 6 projects, e.g. bionic_finger, drone_foldable_wings, fpv_wing; catchable at rough). Would catch it: the hard_stop and clearance_min requirements swept past the stop on the rough parts, with the servo's real travel in project.yaml.
- [wiring] Check every lead is long enough along its routed path (plus slack) when the route is first drawn. (13x in 6 projects, e.g. drone_foldable_wings, fpv_5in, pan_tilt; catchable at detail). Would catch it: the wiring length check against the routed path, run when the routes are first declared.
- [motion/assembly] Walk the assembly order on the rough parts: every part must reach its place along a free path (insert paths, horn and servo order). (9x in 6 projects, e.g. bionic_finger, fpv_5in, fpv_wing; catchable at rough). Would catch it: insertion_paths / horn_paths (kerf/access.py) and the ASSEMBLY.md order run on the rough parts.
- [strength] Set infill, walls and minimum wall thickness for each part from its load when the part is first drawn, not at review. (8x in drone_foldable_wings, fpv_wing, robotic_gripper_claw; catchable at rough). Would catch it: the infill check (kerf/infill.py) and the wall-thickness check run on the rough parts.
- [wiring] Route every wire through holes and channels that exist; check the routes as soon as the parts they cross are real. (3x in drone_foldable_wings, fpv_5in; catchable at detail). Would catch it: the wiring route check in `kerf check` run after each part group is drawn, not after all of them.
